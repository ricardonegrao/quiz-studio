Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Controller: Ricardo Negrão · Contact: ricardonegrao@gmail.com

Quiz Studio IA (“the app”) is a desktop application that runs on the user's own computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, where it is stored and how to delete it.

1. Data accessed on connected platforms

The app only accesses a platform after you connect your account and grant permissions on the platform's official consent screen.

  • TikTok
    • user.info.basic: account identifier (open_id), display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
    • video.upload: send the video you selected to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
    • video.publish: publish the video you selected directly to your profile, using the privacy level and options you choose.
    • video.list (optional): read your public video list and statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) to show the performance of videos posted through the app.
  • YouTube (Google)
    • youtube.upload: upload the video you selected to your channel with the title, description, visibility and disclosures you choose.
    • youtube.readonly: read your channel name and thumbnail and the statistics of videos uploaded through the app.

The app does not read messages, followers, third-party comments or any other account data beyond what is described above.

2. Where data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on your computer, in the app's data folder. The developer does not operate any server that receives, stores or processes your accounts, videos or statistics.

3. Third-party services used by the app

  • Anthropic (Claude): receives the quiz topic, settings and question text to generate and fact-check content. It does not receive any TikTok or YouTube account data.
  • Microsoft (Edge) text-to-speech: receives narration text to produce audio.
  • Wikimedia Commons, Pexels or Pixabay (optional): receive image search terms.
  • TikTok and Google/YouTube: receive the videos and posting data you send, under their own policies.

The app's use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Use of TikTok is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service.

4. How data is used

Data is used only for the functions described above. The app does not sell data, show ads, build user profiles or share data with third parties beyond the services listed in section 3. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your computer until you disconnect the account or delete the app's data folder.
  • The app's Disconnect button revokes access on the platform and deletes the token from your computer.
  • You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok's security settings and at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Children

The app is a tool for content creators and is not directed to children. Connected accounts must meet each platform's minimum age requirements.

7. Security

Credentials stay on the user's computer and communication with the platforms uses HTTPS.

8. Your rights

You may request information, correction or deletion through the contact below. Because data is stored on your own computer, deletion is immediate when you disconnect your accounts and delete the app's data folder.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the current version. Questions: ricardonegrao@gmail.com.
